# Omnio v0.6.1-alpha

**Polish pass — 2026-07-25**

A full walk through every major flow as a first-time user would experience it,
fixing what that walk exposed. No new capabilities; this release is entirely
about the quality of what already exists.

> **Alpha.** Interfaces and data shapes may change between pre-releases. Personal
> mode has no authentication by design — do not expose an instance holding data
> you are unwilling to make public.

## ✨ Improved

- **PDFs show their first page** wherever a file appears — in your files, in the
  details panel, and in quick look. They previously showed a blank icon, which
  made a folder of documents impossible to tell apart at a glance.
- **Quick look works for PDFs.** Pressing space on one used to open an empty
  white panel.
- **Previews sit on a page edge**, so a white document reads as paper rather
  than as empty space.
- **The details panel** opens at the top of a file's details, and its empty state
  now says what it is waiting for.
- **Toolbar presentation**: the sort control no longer wraps onto two lines, and
  the thumbnail size control matches the view switcher beside it.

## 🐞 Fixed

- The **"Open with"** list showed internal names such as `pdf-merge` instead of
  "Merge PDFs".
- File previews could vanish entirely and leave a broken image in their place.

## Verified

193 build, lint, typecheck and unit tasks, and 61 browser tests — including new
cover for the two defects that could silently return: previews must be real
images for both pictures and PDFs, and tool names must never be replaced by
internal identifiers.
